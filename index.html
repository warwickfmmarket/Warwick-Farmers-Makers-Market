Key terms at a glance

  • Set up before 7am. No vehicles moving in customer walkways 7am–12pm.
  • Park inside Queens Park, not on Alice Street. Keep vehicles 3 m from the bitumen road.
  • Bring your own marquee, table and chairs. Weight your stall down securely.
  • Only approved products may be sold. Ask the committee in writing to add new ones.
  • Food, egg and meat sellers must meet Southern Downs Regional Council local laws.
  • Take your rubbish with you. Dogs on leads at all times.

The full Terms & Conditions are in the Vendors Handbook, available inside the Vendor Hub.
